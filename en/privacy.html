Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

1. What we collect

Yorinuki needs no account. The developer collects nothing: no photos, analysis results, locations or usage. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

2. Access to Photos

Yorinuki reads your photo library to compare look-alikes, find blurry shots and add up sizes. Photos and results stay on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere.

3. How photos are analysed

Comparing photos, rating how they look and judging faces use Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone. Nobody is identified.

4. Where data is kept

The analysis, your review progress and protected photos are kept only on this iPhone.

5. Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple; the developer never sees payment details.

6. Deleting data

Settings > Delete App Data removes the app's data. Photos in the Photos app are not deleted.
