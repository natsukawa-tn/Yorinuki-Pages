Help

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Can photos be deleted without me knowing?

No. Photos are deleted only when you tap Delete on your iPhone's confirmation.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes. They stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days. Restore them from there.

How is the best shot picked?

By focus, Apple's rating of how a photo looks, and when people are in it, how their faces came out (eyes open, not blurred). A favorite always wins. You can also touch and hold a photo and choose Make This the Best.

A photo I want to keep keeps being suggested

Touch and hold it and choose Protect: it is never suggested again. Photos you made favorites in the Photos app are never suggested either.

The analysis takes a while

On the first run, likely look-alikes are analysed one by one, newest first, so you can start with what is found. Nothing is analysed while the app is closed, and it slows down if your iPhone gets warm.

What does the free version do?

Month-by-month review, big videos and screenshots are unlimited. Look-alikes: 3 groups a day. Premium (one purchase) makes look-alikes unlimited and adds Delete All Suggestions and blurry shots.

Only some photos show up

With Limited Access only the photos you picked are visible. In the Settings app, open Yorinuki > Photos and choose Full Access.
